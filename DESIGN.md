## 2. Personality & Design Direction (Refined Editorial & Modern Engineering)
- **Aesthetic**: Understated Technical Craft & Low-Fatigue Contrast (Soft Charcoal Slate + Calming Royal Slate Blue).
- **Tone**: Grounded, human, authoritative, evidence-driven, clear, and tactile.
- **Anti-Slop Discipline**:
  - Rejection of eye-straining high contrast (harsh pitch black `#000000`/`#0A0D14` paired with blinding neon electric cyan `#38BDF8`).
  - Rejection of generic AI slop: fake blinking status LEDs (`animate-pulse`), decorative colored left stripes, artificial background blueprints (`.tech-grid`), and fake grayscale photo filters.
  - Authentic visual assets in true, natural color: real project screenshots (`/projects/*.webp`) and real profile photograph (`/assets/profile.jpg`).
  - Tactile physical press feedback (`active:scale-[0.98]`) on all buttons and interactive cards.
  - Snappy, purposeful animations (`duration-200 ease-out`), no sluggish delays or perpetual looping movements.

## 3. The Three Dials
- **DESIGN_VARIANCE**: 5 (Balanced, structured, architectural, clear grid rhythm)
- **MOTION_INTENSITY**: 3 (Subtle, purposeful transitions with instant tactile feedback; no endless loop animations)
- **VISUAL_DENSITY**: 4 (Balanced breathing room with high legibility and low optical fatigue)

## 4. Color Palette
- **Canvas / Foundation**: Soft Charcoal (`#0e1015`)
- **Surface / Cards**: Clean Layered Surface (`#15181f` / `#1c202a`)
- **Card Hover / Active**: Elevated Surface (`#232835`)
- **Borders & Dividers**: Delicate Slate (`border-white/[0.08]` / `border-white/[0.06]`)
- **Text Primary**: Soft Paper (`#f1f3f7`), readable without stark ocular glare
- **Text Secondary / Muted**: Muted Slate (`#949db0`)
- **Single Locked Accent**: Calming Royal Slate Blue (`#4b85db` / `#3b73c4`)

## 5. Typography
- **Headings & Body**: Plus Jakarta Sans (`font-sans`), tight tracking (`-0.02em`), sharp modern geometry.
- **Technical & Metrics**: JetBrains Mono (`font-mono`) for dates, credentials, IPK, tags, and code elements.
