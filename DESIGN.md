# DESIGN.md

## 1. Identity & Purpose
- **Subject**: Adit Hardiansyah Surachman — Full-stack Developer & Network Security Specialist.
- **Location**: Bandung, Indonesia.
- **Core Narrative**: Practical, evidence-based software and systems builder with real, deployed projects (GadgetVault, Surabi Cikal Cisangkan, Sistem Kas Masjid AT-Tijaniyah, Komik Zynqtoon, SaveTok) and verified credentials (S1 Teknik Informatika STMIK Mardira Indonesia IPK 3.61, Google Cybersecurity, BNSP).

## 2. Personality & Mood
- **Aesthetic**: Minimalist Editorial.
- **Tone**: Human, grounded, articulate, and structured like a curated design/technical publication.
- **Key Characteristics**:
  - High typographical contrast with intentional scale.
  - Generous, structured whitespace with precise editorial gridlines.
  - Strict absence of AI clichés: zero neon blur blobs, no floating pill cards, no fake stats.
  - Every interactive element is concrete, tactile, and resilient.

## 3. Liveliness Dials
- **ENERGY**: 2 (Balanced, clear, modern editorial pace)
- **RHYTHM**: 2 (Structured layout rhythm with deliberate pacing between sections)
- **MOTION**: 1 (Purposeful micro-interactions, subtle state transitions, no gratuitous bouncing or decorative float loops)

## 4. Color Palette
- **Canvas / Background**: Warm Charcoal (`#121110` / `#181716`)
- **Surface / Containers**: Refined Stone Dark (`#1C1B19` / `#242220`)
- **Borders & Dividers**: Delicate warm hairline borders (`rgba(245, 240, 230, 0.1)`)
- **Text Primary**: Warm Paper / Off-White (`#F5F2EB`)
- **Text Secondary / Muted**: Muted Warm Stone (`#A8A29E`)
- **Accent**: Refined Terracotta / Warm Ochre (`#D96B43` or `#E2583E`), applied sparingly for focus and active states.

## 5. Typography
- **Headings**: Editorial font with sharp proportions, tight tracking (`tracking-tight`), and high scale contrast.
- **Body**: Clean, legible sans-serif with natural reading line-height (`leading-relaxed`).
- **Technical & Meta**: Tabular monospace for dates, metrics, tech stacks, and specifications.

## 6. Layout & Composition
- Editorial grid layouts with asymmetric balance.
- In-depth project case studies with concrete problem, solution, and impact.
- Clean, accessible navigation and footer without boilerplate filler.
